About Me 🌱

I am a BSc. Hons. Agriculture graduate with a clear and purposeful mission to contribute to a world where no one goes hungry and where the land that feeds us is protected for generations to come.

Everything I do is oriented toward two of the world's most important frameworks for global agricultural transformation: the United Nations SDG 2 and the FAO Strategic Framework 2022–2031.

My mission in one sentence

"To apply integrated management practices and data-driven technology to protect crops from pests and diseases, and to restore and sustain soil health — advancing both Better Production and a Better Environment toward a food-secure and ecologically resilient world."

My academic foundation spans Agronomy, Horticulture, Soil Science, Plant Pathology, Entomology, Plant Breeding, Agricultural Engineering, and Agricultural Extension, giving me the depth to understand these problems from the ground up. I am currently aspiring toward a Master's degree to build the specialized research expertise needed to contribute at an institutional scale.

I’ve realized that when agricultural science pairs with technology, problems that once took seasons to detect can be caught in hours. By upskilling in Python, R, SQL, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Bioinformatics, I am building the capacity to design intelligent systems that extend the reach of good practice beyond traditional limits.

"Every project in this portfolio is a step toward that goal—rooted in science, powered by data, and oriented toward a food-secure and ecologically resilient world."
